Calzoni

A calzone is an oven-baked, folded pizza that has sealed edges so all the good stuff stays in! Anything that goes on a pizza can go into a calzone! The pizza fillings are placed on one half of the circular shaped pizza dough. The dough is then folded over, crimped, coated with an egg wash or a brush of olive oil and baked in the oven.

INGREDIENS

  • 500 g all-purpose flour
  • 300 ml lukewarm water
  • 10 g salt
  • 3 g dry yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 250 g ricotta
  • 150 g well-drained mozzarella, cubed
  • 100 g cooked ham, diced
  • Black pepper
  • Peanut or vegetable oil

~ The Method ~

  1. Dissolve the yeast and sugar in the lukewarm water and let it rest for 5 minutes.
  2. Pour the flour into a bowl, add the yeast water, and knead for a few minutes. Add the salt and keep kneading until the dough is smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover and let rise for 2 hours, until doubled in size.
  4. Make the filling by mixing the ricotta, mozzarella, ham, and a few grinds of black pepper.
  5. Divide the dough into 4 balls and roll each one out into a disc about 20 cm (8 inches) wide.

  6. Spoon the filling onto one half of each disc, leaving the edge free.

  7. Fold the dough over into a half-moon and seal the edges well by pressing with your fingers (or with the tines of a fork).
  8. Heat plenty of oil to 170-180 °C (340-355 °F) in a deep, heavy-bottomed pot.
  9. Fry the calzones one or two at a time for 3-4 minutes per side, until puffed and golden.
  10. Drain on paper towels, season with a little salt, and serve hot.
