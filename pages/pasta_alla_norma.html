Pasta alla norma

Pasta alla Norma, also called pasta con le melanzane ('pasta with eggplant'), is a pasta dish made from eggplant. It is typical of Sicilian cuisine, from Catania in particular.

INGREDIENS

  • Marinara Sauce
  • Eggplant
  • Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
  • Pasta Rigatoni
  • Fresh Basil
  • Ricotta Salata

~ The Method ~

  1. Build the marinara: gently sweat sliced garlic in good extra-virgin olive oil over low heat until fragrant and barely golden. Add crushed San Marzano tomatoes, a pinch of salt, and a basil stem, then simmer for 15 to 20 minutes until the sauce is glossy and lightly reduced. Remove the basil stem and keep the sauce covered over the lowest heat.
  2. Peel the eggplant: following Ottolenghi's technique, use a vegetable peeler to remove lengthwise strips of skin, leaving alternating bands of peel for a zebra-striped effect. This keeps some of the skin's texture without stringy peel in the finished dish.
  3. Roast the eggplant: slice into even rounds about 1.5 cm (0.5 in) thick, brush both sides generously with olive oil, and season with fine salt. Roast on a lined tray at 220°C (425°F), turning once, until deeply golden and tender, about 25 to 30 minutes.
  4. Cook the pasta: bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and season it well, until it tastes lightly of the sea. Cook the pasta 1 to 2 minutes short of the package time so it finishes in the sauce.
  5. Reserve the pasta water: just before draining, set aside a cup of the starchy cooking water, as its starch binds the sauce to the pasta. If you forget, a splash of hot tap water works in a pinch.

  6. Marry the pasta and sauce: transfer the drained pasta to the pan with the marinara and toss over medium heat, adding splashes of pasta water until the sauce clings and turns silky.

  7. Finish the dish: off the heat, fold in torn fresh basil, most of the grated ricotta salata (reserving some for garnish), and a pinch of chili or other spices if desired. Gently fold in the roasted eggplant, letting some pieces break down into the sauce for body.
  8. Plate and serve: taste and adjust the salt, then plate in warm bowls and garnish with the reserved cheese and a few small basil leaves. Serve immediately. Buon appetito.
